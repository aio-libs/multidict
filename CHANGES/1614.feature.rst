Stopped GCC from spending its inlining budget on view and iterator slots
and on module initialization, and forced the key-identity helper inline
everywhere, which on GCC 13 made ``del d[key]`` about 8 percent and
``key in d`` up to 6 percent faster on free-threaded builds, and
``update()`` about 3.5 percent faster on regular ones
-- by :user:`asvetlov`.
