Stopped taking an extra reference to the key when a compact
:class:`~multidict.MultiDict` entry is deleted, made ``popitem()`` return
such an entry's stored key without rebuilding it, and gave
:class:`~multidict.CIMultiDict` its own ``getall()``, cutting
``popitem()`` by about 4% and ``pop()`` by about 4% on both builds, and
``del d[key]`` by about 3% on the GIL build
-- by :user:`asvetlov`.
