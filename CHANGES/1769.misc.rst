Stopped the C extension from computing a key's identity for
:meth:`~multidict.MultiDict.add`, :meth:`~multidict.MultiDict.getall`,
``md[key] = value`` and ``del md[key]`` when the key, or an
:class:`~multidict.istr`'s canonical form, already serves as one, as
``md[key]`` did: on the GIL build ``del md[key]`` needed 4% to 5% fewer
instructions and ``md[key] = value`` about 2% fewer
-- by :user:`asvetlov`.
