Fixed :meth:`~multidict.MultiDict.extend`, :meth:`~multidict.MultiDict.update`
and :meth:`~multidict.MultiDict.merge` reserving room for the new items by
comparing table sizes, which ignored the room taken by deleted entries: a
table short of room only because of deletions was grown by the adds instead
of compacted. The reservation checked the free room and sized the table by
live entries instead, in both implementations -- by :user:`asvetlov`.
