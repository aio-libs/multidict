Replaced ``Py_CLEAR()`` on the C extension's success paths with
``Py_DECREF()`` or ``Py_XDECREF()`` wherever nothing reads the variable
afterwards. Error paths kept ``Py_CLEAR()``. Building a multidict from
items became up to 1.7% faster, and comparing one with another mapping
up to 4% faster -- by :user:`asvetlov`.
